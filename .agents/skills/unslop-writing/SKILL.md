# Unslop Writing Framework: Banishing AI Tropes

Large language models naturally gravitate towards predictable rhetorical crutches, hollow hype words, and pseudo-profound moralizing wrap-ups. This skill defines explicit rules to audit, strip, and rewrite scripts into crisp, grounded, human prose.

---

## 1. Forbidden AI Phrases & Structural Slop

### A. The "Not Just X, But Y" Antithesis Trap
- ❌ **Slop**: *"Ini bukan sekadar mainan, melainkan cerminan gengsi dan validasi sosial kita di era digital."*
- ❌ **Slop**: *"Ini bukan lagi soal secangkir kopi, tapi tentang gaya hidup generasi urban."*
- ✅ **Human Rewrite**: State the concrete fact directly: *"Orang membelinya sebagai tiket masuk circle sosial."*

### B. The Pseudo-Profound Moralizing Conclusion
AI habitually tacks on an unearned philosophical reflection at the end of scripts.
- ❌ **Slop**: *"Dan pada akhirnya, kita belajar bahwa di era media sosial, tren datang dan pergi, namun FOMO akan selalu abadi."*
- ❌ **Slop**: *"Semua fenomena gila ini mengingatkan kita untuk lebih bijak mengelola hasrat konsumtif."*
- ✅ **Rule**: **ABSOLUTELY NO MORALIZING CONCLUSIONS**. End on:
  1. A concrete operational fact or number.
  2. An ironic real-world twist or unresolved investigation detail.
  3. A sharp business payoff.

### C. Synthetic Transition Crutches
- ❌ **Ban**: *"Kuncinya berawal dari..."*
- ❌ **Ban**: *"Seketika, demam meledak di seluruh penjuru..."*
- ❌ **Ban**: *"Siapa sangka, di balik kesuksesan tersebut..."*
- ❌ **Ban**: *"Namun, cerita tidak berhenti di situ..."*
- ❌ **Ban**: *"Menariknya...", "Uniknya...", "Usut punya usut..."*
- ✅ **Human Flow**: Use direct causal transitions (*"Tapi ada masalah matematika di sini:"*, *"Strateginya dimulai dari..."*, *"Triknya ada di buku menu:"*).

### D. Cheap TikTok Hype Adjectives
- ❌ **Ban**: *"Auto kalap"*, *"Gila banget"*, *"Bikin geleng-geleng kepala"*, *"Sangat jenius"*, *"Mencengangkan"*.
- ✅ **Rule**: Show the scale using specific metrics, actions, or physical evidence rather than telling the viewer how to feel.

---

## 2. The Unslop Checklist

Before approving any script beat:
- [ ] **Zero Rhetorical Fillers**: Does the beat start directly with a noun, action, or concrete statement?
- [ ] **No Unearned Preaching**: Is the conclusion a statement of fact or future implication, without life advice?
- [ ] **Asymmetrical Sentences**: Vary sentence structures instead of repeating `[Intro Clause], [Main Clause]`.
- [ ] **Physical Specifics**: Did we use specific names, locations, and numbers instead of vague generalities?
- [ ] **Read-Aloud Test**: Would a credible documentary host or investigative journalist say this on camera without cringing?
