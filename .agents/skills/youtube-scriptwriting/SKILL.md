# YouTube Video Essay Scriptwriting Framework (Short-Form & Explainer)

Unlike TikTok which relies on ephemeral trend sounds and hyper-staccato screaming, YouTube audiences demand **substance, investigative curiosity, and intellectual satisfaction**.

---

## 1. The Documentary Curiosity Architecture (60s)

A YouTube short-form video essay functions as a compressed investigative report:

```
[0s - 4s]   ──► The Concrete Anomaly (A real-world paradox or baffling number)
[4s - 15s]  ──► The Physical Evidence (Show the scene, the receipt, the queue, or the headline)
[15s - 35s] ──► The Business/Psychological Engine (How the math or system actually works)
[35s - 50s] ──► The Grand Reveal / Escalation (The counter-intuitive discovery or margin secret)
[50s - 60s] ──► The Hard-Hitting Landing (The real consequence or strategic reality)
```

### Stage 1: The Concrete Anomaly (0–4s)
- Drop straight into an economic or behavioral contradiction.
- *Example*: *"Di saat semua restoran menaikkan harga karena inflasi, gerai mie ini justru menjual satu porsi komplit seharga sepuluh ribu rupiah."*

### Stage 2: The Physical Evidence (4–15s)
- Cite verified real-world artifacts: a viral post, Google Maps queue, receipt, or store counter.
- *Example*: *"Hasilnya: ratusan driver ojol rela antre dua jam di bawah hujan, cuma buat pesanan senilai tiga puluh ribu."*

### Stage 3: The Engine / The Mechanism (15–35s)
- Explain the underlying business model or psychological mechanism.
- Avoid abstract praise ("mereka sangat pintar"). Explain the literal playbook:
  - *Example*: *"Mie murah itu sebenarnya cuma umpan. Margin utama mereka bukan dari mie, tapi dari minuman manis dengan keuntungan lebih dari tujuh puluh persen."*

### Stage 4: The Grand Reveal (35–50s)
- Hit the viewer with the unexpected metric or operational scale using a dramatic visual pop.
- *Example*: *"Satu outlet bisa memproduksi dua ribu porsi sehari, memutar kursi pelanggan setiap dua puluh menit."*

### Stage 5: The Hard-Hitting Landing (50–60s)
- End on the concrete business conclusion or lingering market implication.
- Zero life lessons. Zero moralizing.
- *Example*: *"Mereka tidak menjual makanan mewah--mereka membangun pabrik perputaran volume tercepat di Indonesia."*

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Rules

1. **Investigative Calm**: Speak with the quiet confidence of an analyst uncovering an open secret.
2. **Concrete Nouns & Verbs**: Replace vague adjectives with nouns:
   - Instead of *"sangat menguntungkan"*, write *"menghasilkan margin kotor 70%"*.
   - Instead of *"antrean luar biasa"*, write *"antrean dua jam melingkari trotoar"*.
3. **Pacing for Cadence**:
   - Use commas (`,`) for structured breath pauses.
   - Use colons (`:`) to introduce revelations.
   - Keep word count strictly between **120 and 138 words** for a natural 55–58s delivery.
