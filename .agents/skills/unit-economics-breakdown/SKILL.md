# Unit Economics & Financial Teardown Skill

Use this specialized skill when the video topic analyzes a business model, franchise, corporate balance sheet, or commercial operation (e.g. F&B franchises, retail chains, rental properties, tech platforms).

Do **NOT** force this framework onto cultural, historical, political, or social narrative topics where unit economics are irrelevant.

---

## 1. The Forensic Financial Decomposition

When breaking down a business, always move from the **topline illusion** to the **bottomline reality**:

```
[Retail Price / Omzet] 
       │
       ├──► COGS (Raw materials, packaging, direct wastage)
       │      └── Gross Margin (Often falsely claimed as "70%+ untung!")
       │
       ├──► Direct OPEX (Storefront rent, baristas/staff, electricity/AC, water)
       │      └── Store-Level Contribution Margin
       │
       ├──► Hidden Leakage (Platform commissions 20–30%, wastage, promo discounts)
       │      └── Realized Unit Margin
       │
       └──► Capital Payback (Initial fit-out, machinery, franchise fee amortized)
              └── Net Owner Cash Flow
```

## 2. Concrete Indonesian Numbers & Real-World Anchors

1. **Exact Rupiah Naming**:
   - Never say *"bahan bakunya murah"*.
   - Say: *"Modal bubuk kopi dan susu kental manisnya cuma empat ribu perak. Dijual dua puluh ribu, margin kotornya delapan puluh persen."*
2. **The Hidden Operating Trap**:
   - Cite specific Indonesian operational realities:
     - Biaya sewa ruko Jakarta/Surabaya: *120–250 juta per tahun (wajib bayar di muka 2 tahun)*
     - Daya listrik komersial: *Tarif B2/B3, beban AC pendingin ruangan nonstop 12 jam*
     - Potongan komisi food delivery (Gofood/ShopeeFood/GrabFood): *20% hingga 25% + service charge*
     - Gaji barista/staf: *UMR lokal per kepala + tunjangan*
3. **The Break-Even Volume Equation**:
   - Translate abstract finances into a daily physical goal:
     - *"Biar impas bayar sewa dan listrik, mereka wajib ngejual minimal 150 cup sehari sebelum jam lima sore. Cup ke-151 baru jadi duit pemilik."*

---

## 3. Script Integration Guidelines

- **Conversational Tone**: Explain numbers like a savvy insider friend warning a friend at a coffee shop:
  - *"Kedengarannya cuan melimpah kan? Tapi tunggu sampai lu hitung biaya sewa ruko dan potongan komisi aplikasi..."*
- **Visual Staging Cue**:
  - Pair financial breakdowns with progressive visual callouts (e.g. hero coffee cup appearing first, followed 2 seconds later by cost badges popping in around it).
