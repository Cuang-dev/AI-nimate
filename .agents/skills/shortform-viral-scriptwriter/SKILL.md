# Viral Short-Form Scriptwriting & Cadence Engineering Guide

This skill provides an authoritative, battle-tested framework for generating viral 60-second video essay scripts optimized for high retention on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. It also defines how to engineer speech **cadence, micro-pauses, and breath rhythms** specifically for Neural AI TTS engines (like Edge-TTS or ElevenLabs).

---

## 1. The 60-Second Retention Curve Architecture

Short-form algorithmic retention drops 40% in the first 3 seconds if the hook is weak. Every script must follow the **5-Stage Narrative Loop**:

```
[0s - 3s]   ──► The Pattern-Interrupt Hook (High Curiosity Gap / Paradox)
[3s - 12s]  ──► The Stakes & Tension (Why the viewer should care right now)
[12s - 35s] ──► The Mechanism / The Secret (The psychological or strategic 'Why')
[35s - 50s] ──► The Escalation & Chaos (Insane numbers, absurdity, peak climax)
[50s - 60s] ──► The Punchline & Seamless Loop (Satisfying takeaway + loop to start)
```

### Stage 1: The 0–3s Hook (Pattern Interrupt)
- **Forbidden**: "Halo guys", "Di video kali ini", "Tahukah kamu", or greeting the audience.
- **Formula**: `[Shocking Contrast / Paradox]` + `[Absurd Real-World Consequence]`.
- *Example*: *"Boneka bergigi monster ini... sukses bikin ribuan orang di Jakarta rela antre dari jam 4 subuh!"*

### Stage 2: Stakes & Tension (3s – 12s)
- Immediately prove the claim with high-contrast social proof or numbers.
- Highlight the friction or human emotion (anger, jealousy, disbelief, frenzy).
- *Example*: *"Bukan cuma antre, mereka bahkan sampai baku hantam di mall cuma demi gantungan kunci berbulu ini!"*

### Stage 3: The Hidden Mechanism (12s – 35s)
- Unpack the psychological or business machinery behind the trend.
- Break down the 2 pillars: **The Catalyst** (e.g. Celebrity status signal) and **The Addiction Loop** (e.g. Blind Box dopamine gambling).
- Use conversational Indonesian signposts: *"Gini ceritanya...", "Kuncinya ada di dua hal...", "Bukan sembarang mainan..."*

### Stage 4: The Escalation & Chaos (35s – 50s)
- Rapid-fire numbers and exponential multiplication.
- Transition directly to visual motion graphics (cash ticker, quantity explosions).
- *Example*: *"Harga resmi 300 ribu... langsung digoreng calo tembus 5 juta rupiah per box! Naik lebih dari 1000 persen!"*

### Stage 5: The Punchline & Infinite Loop (50s – 60s)
- Deliver a sharp philosophical or business realization.
- End on a phrase that seamlessly loops back into the opening sentence of the video.
- *Example*: *"Ini bukan lagi soal beli mainan, tapi beli gengsi dan FOMO digital. Dan semua kegilaan ini... berawal dari satu hal:"* -> loops to hook!

## 2. TTS Cadence & Prosody Engineering

Neural TTS engines read flat text monotonously unless punctuation and rhythm are engineered intentionally. Apply these rules:

### A. Punctuation as Temporal Breathing Markers
- **Ellipsis (`...`)**: Forces a **300ms dramatic suspense pause**. Use right before revealing a number, twist, or name.
  - *Text*: *"Harganya bukan 300 ribu... tapi tembus 5 juta rupiah!"*
- **Comma (`,`)**: Creates a **120ms natural breath pause**. Use to break compound clauses into digestible rhythmic bites.
  - *Text*: *"Di Gandaria City, ratusan orang dorong-dorongan, security kewalahan."*
- **Em Dash (`--`)**: Creates an abrupt **immediate mid-sentence pivot**.
  - *Text*: *"Mereka bukan beli boneka--mereka beli tiket masuk circle sosial!"*
- **Exclamation Mark (`!`)**: Increases pitch energy and urgency on verbs and punchlines.
- **Question Mark (`?`)**: Natural rising vocal inflection for curiosity hooks.

### B. Word Count & Sentence Staccato
- **Sentence Length**: 6 to 11 words maximum per sentence. Never write a 20-word run-on sentence.
- **Rhythmic Modulation**: Alternate 1 short staccato punch (3-5 words) with 1 flowing explanatory clause (8-10 words):
  - *Short*: *"Gila kan?"*
  - *Flow*: *"Pop Mart sukses manfaatin efek dopamine dari sistem blind box."*
  - *Short*: *"Buka box, deg-degan, ketagihan!"*

### C. Phonetic Writing for Indonesian Numbers & Acronyms
- For natural TTS delivery, write numbers clearly:
  - Use clean numerical units: *"300 ribu rupiah"*, *"5 juta rupiah"*, *"1.000 orang"*.
  - For English acronyms or celebrity names, write standard casing so Edge-TTS pronounces them properly: *"Lisa BLACKPINK"*, *"Pop Mart"*, *"FOMO"*, *"QRIS"*.

## 3. Audio-to-Visual Beat Synchronization Rules

1. **One Thought = One Visual Beat**:
   - Each visual scene must last between **2.0 and 4.2 seconds**. Never let a static visual sit on screen for 5+ seconds.
2. **Visual Format Pairing**:
   - When a number or price is spoken -> Trigger `cash-ticker` or `quantity-surge`.
   - When a brand is spoken -> Trigger `brand-spotlight`.
   - When a landmark or mall is spoken -> Trigger `landmark-focus`.
   - When abstract/historical context is spoken -> Trigger `ai-editorial-scene` (Nano Banana).
   - When comparison is made -> Trigger `versus-split`.
3. **Bottom Subtitle Formatting**:
   - Boxless, crisp light text with 1.5px black outline and karaoke word-level highlight.

## 4. Short-Form Script Checklist

Before handing off a script to the director agent, verify:
- [ ] First sentence drops the viewer straight into the action in < 3 seconds (zero fluff).
- [ ] Contains at least 2 high-contrast numeric moments (for `cash-ticker` and `quantity-surge`).
- [ ] Uses punctuation (`...`, `,`, `!`) to control TTS cadence and breathing.
- [ ] Word count is between **130 and 155 words** for a crisp 55–60 second duration.
- [ ] Ending delivers a punchline or seamless loop.
