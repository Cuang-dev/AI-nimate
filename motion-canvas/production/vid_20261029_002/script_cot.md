## [CoT Step 5: Phonetic & Acronym Engineering]

To guarantee 100% natural TTS prosody with zero mispronunciations, stuttering, or synthetic inflection artifacts, all acronyms and corporate tickers are engineered specifically for text-to-speech engines:

1. **`AI` → `A.I.`:**
   - Phonetic Target: `/eɪ aɪ/` ("ay-eye").
   - Punctuation Rule: Formatted as `A.I.` with periods in `tts_transcript`. Prevents TTS engines from blending letters or confusing the acronym with the artificial word "aie".
2. **`OpenAI` → `Open A.I.`:**
   - Phonetic Target: `/ˈoʊpən eɪ aɪ/` ("Open Ay-Eye").
   - Spacing Rule: Formatted with a space and periods to guarantee crisp syllable separation.
3. **`AWS` → `A.W.S.`:**
   - Phonetic Target: `/eɪ ˈdʌbəl.juː ɛs/` ("Ay-Double-U-Ess").
   - Punctuation Rule: Periods inserted between each letter to enforce three distinct phonetic beats.
4. **`GPU` / `GPUs` → `G.P.U.` / `G.P.U.s`:**
   - Phonetic Target: `/dʒiː piː juː/` ("Jee-Pee-Yoo") and `/dʒiː piː juːz/`.
   - Formatted as `G.P.U.` / `G.P.U.s` to eliminate slurred pronunciation.
5. **Numbers & Dollar Amounts:**
   - Spoken text writes out all numbers conversationally (`"three hundred and twenty billion dollars"`, `"six-hundred-billion-dollar formula"`, `"forty-five billion"`, `"five-hundred-billion-dollar revenue chasm"`, `"twenty twenty-five and twenty twenty-six"`).
   - This prevents the TTS engine from reading "$320B" as "dollar three-two-zero-bee" or "2025" as "two thousand twenty-five".
6. **Hardware & Architecture Proper Nouns:**
   - `Blackwell` (`/ˈblækwɛl/`) and `Rubin` (`/ˈruːbɪn/`) pronounced with crisp standard English phonetics.
   - `Sequoia` (`/sɪˈkwɔɪ.ə/`) articulated with deliberate syllable stress.
7. **Pacing & Breath Markers:**
   - Triple dots (`...`) are utilized in `tts_transcript` at key conceptual breaks (e.g., `"what is actually inflating..."`, `"called round-tripping..."`, `"six full years..."`) to force 350ms–500ms acoustic breath pauses in neural voice synthesis models.
